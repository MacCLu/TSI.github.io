Common questions

Before you book.

Which areas does TSI serve?

TSI handles shipments from Goa to selected destinations in Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat and Tamil Nadu. Share your PIN codes to check your route.

Can I ask about a different type of cargo?

Yes. Share the goods type, packaging and any special handling requirements so TSI can discuss whether it can take the shipment.

Can I enquire about carton storage?

Yes. Share the number of cartons, preferred location and how long you need storage. TSI can confirm available space, including at its Bengaluru branch.

How is cargo insured?

TSI arranges insurance for the load. Ask us about the coverage, exclusions and limits for your shipment before dispatch.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Include pickup and delivery locations, the cargo type, package count, approximate weight and dimensions, and your preferred pickup date.
